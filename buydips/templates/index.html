Questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free while buyDips is in beta.

How often are prices checked?

About every 20 minutes on weekdays, covering Asian, Indian, European and US market hours. Prices can be delayed, so treat alerts as a heads-up, not a trading signal.

How many emails will I get?

At most one per check, grouping everything that dipped. Each item alerts once per trading day, even if it keeps falling.

Where does the price data come from?

A third-party source (Yahoo Finance). It can occasionally be wrong or unavailable, which is why alerts are for information only.

Is this investment advice?

No. buyDips only reports that a price moved. It doesn't recommend buying or selling anything.

What do you store about me?

Only your email address and your watchlist. You can delete both permanently from the watchlist page. See the privacy page.
