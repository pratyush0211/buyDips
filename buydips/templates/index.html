Questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes, it's free while buyDips is in beta.

How often are prices checked?

About once an hour on weekdays, sometimes more often. Each market is checked only while it is open and for up to an hour after it closes, to catch the final price. Prices can be delayed, so an email can arrive up to an hour or more after the move. Treat alerts as a heads-up, not a trading signal.

How many emails will I get?

At most one alert email per check, grouping everything that fell. Each item alerts once per trading day, even if it keeps falling. If you use buckets, you also get one email, once for each stock, the first time it is down 10% or more since you added it.

Can I follow gold and silver?

Yes, through two NSE-listed funds, Nippon India ETF Gold BeES and Nippon India Silver ETF. They track the metals and are priced in rupees, so their price differs a little from the spot or MCX price. They follow Indian market hours.

Where does the price data come from?

A third-party source (Yahoo Finance). It can occasionally be wrong or unavailable, which is why alerts are for information only.

Is this investment advice?

No. buyDips only reports that a price moved. It doesn't recommend buying or selling anything, and buyDips is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst.

What do you store about me?

Your email address, your watchlist, your buckets and a record of the alerts we sent you. You can delete all of it permanently with "Delete my account" in Settings. See the privacy page.
