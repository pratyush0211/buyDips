Privacy

A plain-language summary of what buyDips does with your information. Last updated 24 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send alerts.
  • Your watchlist: the items you follow and your alert levels.
  • A record of alerts sent (the stock, the size of the drop, the prices and your alert level), so you aren't emailed twice for the same drop and can see your history in the Alerts tab.

We don't collect your name, phone number, location or payment details. There is no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. That's all.

How it's used

Only to run buyDips: signing you in and sending the alerts you asked for. We don't sell your data or share it for marketing.

Services we rely on

Running buyDips involves these providers, which handle data on our behalf:

  • Render: hosts the website.
  • Supabase: stores accounts and watchlists (database region: Seoul, South Korea).
  • GitHub: runs the scheduled price check.
  • Email delivery: currently Brevo and Google Mail.
  • Yahoo Finance: source of market prices. No personal data is sent to it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of every alert email.
  • Delete everything with the "Delete my account" button on your watchlist page. It permanently removes your email, watchlist and alert history.
